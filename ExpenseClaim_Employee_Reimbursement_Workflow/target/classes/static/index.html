What is this project?

The real-world scenario behind the website

Scenario: An employee spends money for official work and requests reimbursement. The system checks company policy, sends the claim to the assigned manager, and allows Finance to pay only after approval.
EmployeeCreates an itemized claim.
PolicyFlags items above category limits.
ManagerApproves, rejects, or overrides an exception.
FinancePays approved claims and stores the payment reference.
